🗺️ S2-02 Storyboard

What the viewer sees in each beat: camera, motion and the on-screen text added in the edit.

🎬 Clip c1 · 0–7 s

👀 Dark space. A glowing number 9246 drops into a giant glowing table of rows, like a lookup grid. A single dot lights up on its row.

🔤 On-screen text: TOKEN 9246

🎬 Clip c2 · 7–15 s

👀 The single dot splits into streams of glowing numbers that fan out into a 3D coordinate space, axes appearing one after another.

🔤 On-screen text: 1 → 1,536

🎬 Clip c3 · 15–23 s

👀 Hundreds of dots drift and settle into two clusters: warm orange-red fruit dots on the left, gold royal dots on the right, with empty space between.

🔤 On-screen text: FRUIT · ROYALTY

🎬 Clip c4 · 23–31 s

👀 Inside the royalty cluster the dot 'King' glows. An arrow for 'Man' pulls it back, an arrow for 'Woman' pushes it forward.

🔤 On-screen text: KING − MAN + WOMAN

🎬 Clip c5 · 31–38 s

👀 The moving dot lands on a brighter gold dot: Queen. A ring pulses. Hidden inside the dot, a tiny glowing 9246 (rewatch bait).

🔤 On-screen text: = QUEEN

🎬 Clip c6 · 38–45 s

👀 Glowing threads begin to connect distant dots, then everything fades back to the first frame: dark space and one lone dot.

🔤 On-screen text: HOW DO THEY TALK?
