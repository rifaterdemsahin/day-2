🗣️ Voice-over text

Tokens are just numbers. This one, nine-two-four-six, is a word. But to the AI, it means nothing yet. So it gets unfolded. One number becomes fifteen hundred and thirty-six coordinates. And meaning becomes distance. Apples, pears, grapes gather here. Kings, queens, crowns, over there. Now watch. Take king. Subtract man. Add woman. And you land exactly on queen. No one told it. It's just geometry. But how do these vectors talk to each other?
