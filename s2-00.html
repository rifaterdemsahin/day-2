🧭 S2-00 Brief, hook and loop

The promise of the short, the hook and how it loops. Everything else follows from this page.

🧭 Why this short matters (for you, the producer)

🔒 Producer only, not for the audience. Claude's draft from what you told me: correct it, it drives every later decision.

  1. 🔁 Attention is the scarce thing, and the platform measures one question: did they stay, and did they come back?
    At 200%+ watch time every view counts as two. That is why you love the example, and why the loop and the hidden 9246 are the product, not decoration.
  2. 🧠 A viewer can absorb about one new idea in 45 seconds.
    Day 2's single idea: meaning is distance. Embeddings are the bridge from Day 1 (tokens are numbers) to the next question (how do vectors talk). Cut anything that does not serve that one idea.
  3. 🔗 A series compounds; a one-off does not.
    Each short must sell the next one. Day 2 only works if a Day 1 viewer feels the sequel and a new viewer wants Day 3. That is the reason for the teaser and the series tie-in.
  4. ⚙️ You cannot out-spend studios; you can out-iterate them.
    The master spec, the numbered pipeline and the feedback loop make short number 3 cheaper and better than number 2. Every round we log is a lesson reused next time.
  5. ⏳ Your scarce resource is your own time, and Flow is manual.
    Every minute spent in Flow must go to prompts you already approved. So review earliest and cheapest first: script (S2-01), storyboard (S2-02), prompts (S2-03), only then clips (S2-04).
  6. 🏁 Define done before you start.
    Done means: you are proud to post it, the loop feels seamless on replay, the idea lands in one watch and the bait is caught on the second. Anti-goal: beautiful clips that explain nothing, or a first-time view that feels like a loop restart.
🪝 Hook (first second)

Tokens are just numbers. How does an AI know that king minus man plus woman equals queen?

🔄 Loop plan

Last line flows into the first line: '…how do these vectors talk to each other? Tokens are just numbers…'. The last frame is the same dark star-field with a lone glowing dot, which is the first frame.

🥚 Rewatch bait

Token 9246 returns as a tiny glowing dot hidden inside the Queen vector in Scene 4. Only people who watch twice spot it.

🎨 Style line (same on every Flow prompt)

Dark space, deep blue and violet, neon cyan and gold accents, clean glowing lines, cinematic slow camera, vertical 9:16, no text baked into the video (captions are added later), no people, no logos.

🔁 Rewatch rules

  1. ⚡ Hook in the first second.
  2. 🔄 Seamless loop: last line leads into the first.
  3. 🥚 One detail you only see the second time.
  4. 🎞️ A visual change every 2–3 s.
  5. 🏁 Payoff in the last third, teaser last.
  6. 💬 Captions always on, in the safe area.
