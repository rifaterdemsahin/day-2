🛰️ S2-SP Sprite setup

One-time setup of a Fly.io Sprite that has Claude and this repo, so the 🚀 button in the notes panel can fix the site from your notes and redeploy. You run these yourself: they involve your logins and a push token.

🚀 What the notes button does

Open the 📝 panel on any page and press 🚀 Run on sprite. It copies one Terminal command that sends your notes (this page or all pages) to Claude on the sprite day-2. Claude edits the repo, rebuilds the site, updates the spec and iterations, commits, pushes main and checks that Pages is live.

🔒 Why a command and not a direct call: a public page cannot hold a secret. The command runs from your Terminal, with your own Sprites login, so nothing sensitive is on the site.

⚠️ Only your own notes should go in: they become Claude's instructions and it has push rights to this repo.

🔢 Steps to follow (tick each one)

  • 🔁 If the sprite and you both push to main, the command starts with a pull and rebase, so they stay in step.
  • 🧾 Sprite name and folder come from assets/short.json › sprite.
🔁 Update → deploy → feedback. Copy your feedback with 📋 and paste it back to Claude.
