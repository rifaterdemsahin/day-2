🪞 S2-X Sandbox: pride & vanity

💡 In one sentence: take pride, remove what vanity adds, add honor, and you land on virtue.

The King − Man + Woman = Queen trick, replayed with pride and vanity in an in-browser sandbox. Change the words, see the nearest word, and read what the test does and does not prove.

🪞 The idea

King − Man + Woman ≈ Queen works because one direction (male → female) is shared by many word pairs. Does a direction like healthy → hollow exist for pride and vanity? Try vanity − pride + confidence: if the direction is real, the answer should be the hollow twin of confidence (arrogance).

🧭 Reach virtue from vanity and pride

Plain version: vanity and virtue are far apart (they barely point the same way). Pride is already close to virtue. The gap between them is the hollow, applause-hungry part that vanity has and pride does not. So: start at pride, take away what vanity adds, then add one good ingredient. The sandbox tries every ingredient and keeps the ones that land on virtue.

⚠️ What this does and does not prove
  • 🛠️ These are toy vectors I wrote by hand on 5 dimensions (self-regard, hollowness, needs applause, earned, virtue). The arithmetic is real; the language is my judgment. A test that passes here shows the mechanism, not that a real model agrees.
  • 📏 Preset 1 is a near tie: the expected word (arrogance) comes 3rd, within 0.01 of the winner. That is how real analogies often look: right neighbourhood, not always rank 1.
  • 🪤 Untick the box: the answer becomes one of the input words. That is the known trap of the original demo.
  • 🎯 "Virtue" is also one of my five dimensions, so reaching it is partly built in. The honest check is the real-model script, which now includes pride − vanity + honor → virtue.
  • 🔬 Real test: run python3 -I scripts/pride_vanity_real.py (needs pip install gensim and downloads GloVe, about 130 MB). Not run yet.

📖 Reading pride − vanity + honor word by word

  • 🦁 pride = healthy self-respect: you think well of yourself because of something real. High self-regard, high "earned", mostly good.
  • 🪞 vanity = self-regard that lives on looks and applause: high self-regard, but hollow, needs others' admiration, little earned, a bit negative.
  • 🏅 honor = recognition you actually earned, with good character behind it: high "earned", high virtue.
  • 🌿 virtue = being good in a lasting way: high "earned", high virtue, little hollowness.

Why the sum makes sense:

  1. ➖ pride − vanity: both share the same high self-regard, so that part cancels. What is left is what pride has and vanity lacks: earned and virtue up, hollowness and needing applause down. This is the "healthy, not hollow" direction, the same kind of step as man → woman in king − man + woman.
  2. ➕ + honor: honor adds more of exactly what that direction already points to (earned, virtue). So the result becomes pride without the hollow part, plus earned goodness.
  3. 🎯 That is the everyday meaning of virtue: self-respect that is earned, not shown off. The nearest word to the result is virtue, which is why it lands there.
🛠️ Same caveat as above: the numbers are my hand-written toy values, so this explains why the arithmetic works; whether a real model has the same shape is the GloVe test.
